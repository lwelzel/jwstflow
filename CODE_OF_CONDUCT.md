# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

`jwstflow` is intended to be an open, professional, and constructive scientific-software project. Everyone who participates in the project is expected to help maintain an environment in which technical disagreement can be discussed without hostility or intimidation.

This code applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, documentation, and other communication made on behalf of the project.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, methods, or evidence rather than on people;
- assume neither expertise nor lack of expertise from a person's background, seniority, institution, identity, or writing style;
- give technical feedback that is specific enough to be useful;
- accept reasonable review and correction of one's own work;
- acknowledge uncertainty and distinguish evidence from interpretation when discussing scientific or technical questions;
- respect privacy, embargoes, proprietary data, credentials, and other information that is not intended to be public;
- credit contributions appropriately; and
- help make discussions accessible to contributors with different levels of familiarity with the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained personal attacks;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments based on personal characteristics or identity;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- deliberate public disclosure of another person's private information;
- knowingly posting credentials, proprietary data, embargoed material, or other sensitive information without authorization;
- repeated disruption of technical discussions after a maintainer has asked for the behaviour to stop;
- plagiarism or deliberate misrepresentation of authorship, contributions, results, or evidence; and
- retaliation against someone who raises a conduct concern in good faith.

Technical disagreement, critical review, and requests for stronger evidence are not in themselves violations of this code when expressed professionally and in good faith.

## Reporting a problem

Conduct concerns may be reported privately to the project maintainer, Lukas Welzel, at `welzel@strw.leidenuniv.nl`.

Reports should include enough context to understand the concern, such as relevant links, dates, screenshots, or a description of what occurred. Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly unless there is a clear reason to do so.

The maintainer will handle reports as privately as reasonably possible. Information may need to be shared when necessary to investigate a report, protect participants, or comply with applicable institutional or legal obligations.

If the maintainer is personally involved in the reported conduct, a reporter should use an appropriate independent institutional channel instead of relying on the project process.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and context of a violation, project moderation may include:

- a private request to change behaviour;
- editing, hiding, or removing inappropriate material where the platform permits it;
- closing or locking a discussion;
- rejecting a contribution;
- temporary restrictions on participation; or
- permanent removal from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behaviour, consider whether harm is ongoing, and aim to protect the ability of others to participate safely and productively.

Good-faith mistakes should normally be addressed with correction and explanation. Serious harassment, threats, disclosure of sensitive information, or repeated misconduct may justify immediate stronger action.

## Scope of scientific and technical review

Maintainers may decline contributions for reasons unrelated to conduct, including project scope, maintainability, test coverage, scientific validity, compatibility, or duplication of existing functionality. Such technical decisions should be explained in terms of the project rather than presented as judgments about a contributor.

## Changes to this code

This code may be revised as the project and contributor community develop. Material changes should be recorded in the repository history.
